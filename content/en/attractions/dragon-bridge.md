# Dragon Bridge: Da Nang's Fire-Breathing Icon

Dragon Bridge (Cau Rong) is the most recognizable structure in Da Nang. Opened in 2013, the 666 m bridge spans the Han River with a steel dragon that breathes fire and water on weekend nights. It is the centerpiece of Da Nang's riverfront and a must-see for any visitor.

The fire and water show happens every Saturday and Sunday at 21:00. Crowds gather on both banks 30 minutes before. The show lasts about 5 minutes — fire first, then water.

## Location & getting there

Dragon Bridge is in central Da Nang, crossing the Han River between the city center and the beach side.

**From Da Nang airport:** 3 km. Grab 40,000–60,000 VND, 8 min. Or 20-min walk.

**From My Khe Beach:** 2 km. Grab 30,000–50,000 VND, 6 min. Or 25-min walk.

**From Hoi An:** 30 km south. Grab 300,000–450,000 VND, 45 min.

## Highlights not to miss

- **Fire show (21:00 Sat–Sun):** The dragon's head breathes fire 9 times, then water 3 times. Best viewed from the east bank (Tran Hung Dao street) or from a boat on the river.
- **Night illumination:** The bridge is lit with LED lights that change color. The dragon glows gold and red. Best from 19:00–22:00.
- **Han River Bridge (nearby):** The rotating bridge 500 m north. It rotates 90° to let ships pass (weekends 01:00–04:00). Less famous but interesting.
- **Love Lock Bridge:** Small bridge next to Dragon Bridge. Couples attach padlocks. Free to walk.
- **Riverside walk:** The east bank promenade is lit and landscaped. Good for evening strolls.

## Suggested visit plan (1–2 hours)

1. **19:30–20:30:** Arrive at east bank. Walk the promenade. Dinner at riverside restaurant.
2. **20:30–21:00:** Secure a viewing spot near the bridge (east bank, close to the dragon head).
3. **21:00–21:05:** Fire and water show. 5 minutes.
4. **21:05–21:30:** Walk across the bridge. Photos of the lit dragon.
5. **21:30–22:00:** Riverside cafes for drinks. Or river cruise.

**Tip:** The east bank (Tran Hung Dao) is closer to the dragon head. The west bank (Bach Dang) has restaurants with views.

## Practical tips

- **Arrival:** Get to the viewing spot by 20:30. By 20:45 it's packed.
- **Wet zone:** The water show sprays the area near the dragon head. Stand 50+ m back if you don't want to get wet.
- **Photography:** Tripod recommended for night shots. Fire show is bright — fast shutter. Water show: slower shutter for mist effect.
- **Traffic:** Roads near the bridge close 20:30–21:30. Walk or Grab to within 500 m.
- **Food:** Riverside restaurants on Bach Dang street. Prices 80,000–200,000 VND/dish. Book ahead on show nights.
- **Accessibility:** Bridge is flat and accessible. Promenade is paved.

## Frequently asked questions

**Is the fire show every night?**
No. Saturday and Sunday only, at 21:00. Weekdays: no show, but the bridge is lit.

**What time should I arrive?**
By 20:30 for a good viewing spot. The show starts at 21:00 sharp.

**Can I see the show from a boat?**
Yes. River cruises (150,000–250,000 VND) offer a unique perspective from the water.

**Is it suitable for children?**
Yes. Children love the fire and water. Arrive early for a safe viewing spot away from the spray zone.

**What if it rains?**
The show may be cancelled in heavy rain or strong wind. Check local announcements on the day.
