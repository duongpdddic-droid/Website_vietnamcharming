# Nha Trang Beach: 6 km of Sand and Sea

Nha Trang Beach is a 6 km crescent of sand along Tran Phu street in the city center. It is the most accessible beach in Nha Trang — walk out of your hotel and you're there. The water is clear, the sand is fine, and the beach is lined with palm trees, cafes, and water sports operators.

The beach is divided into sections: the central area (near the Sailing Club) is busiest, the northern end (near Po Nagar) is quieter, and the southern end (near the port) is less developed.

## Location & getting there

Nha Trang Beach runs along Tran Phu street, the main coastal road in Nha Trang city center.

**From airport (Cam Ranh):** 40 km south. Taxi/Grab 300,000–400,000 VND, 45–60 min.

**From train station:** 3 km. Grab 50,000–80,000 VND, 8 min.

**From city center hotels:** Walk. Most hotels are within 200 m of the beach.

## Highlights not to miss

- **Swimming:** Calm water, gentle slope. Best in the morning (06:00–09:00) before wind picks up. Lifeguards on duty.
- **Water sports:** Jet ski (500,000 VND/15 min), parasailing (800,000 VND), banana boat (300,000 VND), snorkeling trips (500,000–800,000 VND).
- **Beach volleyball:** Free courts near the Sailing Club. Locals play in the evening.
- **Sunset:** The beach faces east, so sunrise is better than sunset. But the promenade is lit and pleasant at night.
- **Thap Cham (Cham Tower):** Not on the beach, but 2 km north. Combine with a morning swim.
- **Night market:** 500 m inland on Nguyen Thi Minh Khai. Evening food and souvenirs.

## Suggested visit plan (half day)

1. **06:00–07:30:** Morning swim. Water is calmest. Fewer people.
2. **07:30–08:30:** Breakfast at a beachfront cafe (pho, banh mi, coffee).
3. **08:30–10:00:** Water sports — jet ski or parasailing.
4. **10:00–11:00:** Sunbed, read, relax.
5. **11:00–12:00:** Shower, change. Lunch at a seafood restaurant.

**Tip:** The beach is hottest 11:00–15:00. Swim early, hide midday.

## Practical tips

- **Swimming safety:** Swim within the flagged area. Lifeguards on duty 06:00–18:00. Rip currents can occur — check flags.
- **Sun protection:** Hat, sunscreen, umbrella. No shade on the beach. Sunbeds with umbrellas 50,000–100,000 VND/day.
- **Water sports:** Negotiate price before starting. Check equipment condition. Wear life jacket.
- **Money:** ATMs on Tran Phu street. Beach vendors accept cash only.
- **Food:** Beachfront cafes 60,000–150,000 VND/dish. Seafood restaurants 200,000–500,000 VND/person.
- **Cleanliness:** The beach is cleaned daily. Northern end is cleaner than central.

## Frequently asked questions

**Is Nha Trang Beach safe for swimming?**
Yes, in the flagged area with lifeguards on duty. Check flags before entering. Rip currents can occur.

**What is the best time to visit?**
March–September for calm, clear water. October–February can be rough with waves.

**Are there free showers?**
No. Showers and changing rooms cost 5,000–10,000 VND. Some hotels offer free beach access for guests.

**Can I rent a sunbed?**
Yes. 50,000–100,000 VND/day including umbrella. Negotiate with the vendor.

**Is the beach suitable for children?**
Yes. Shallow water, gentle slope. Lifeguards on duty. Water sports operators have age minimums (usually 8+).
