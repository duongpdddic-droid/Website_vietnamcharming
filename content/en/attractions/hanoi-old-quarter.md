# Hanoi Old Quarter: 36 Streets That Still Know Their Name

The Old Quarter is the part of Hanoi that tourists photograph and locals still use. Its 36 streets were organized by guild in the 15th century — silver on Hang Bac, silk on Hang Gai, paper on Hang Ma — and the trade logic survives even where the guilds do not. You can walk one block and change industries.

This guide covers the walkable core: which streets matter, where to eat, how the night market works, and the small rules that keep a visit pleasant rather than chaotic.

## Location & getting there

The Old Quarter sits immediately north of Hoan Kiem Lake. From the lake's north shore, cross the road and you are in it.

**From airport (Noi Bai):** Bus 86 runs every 25–30 minutes, 05:00–21:00, fare 45,000 VND. Journey takes 45–60 minutes. Taxi/Grab: 300,000–450,000 VND, 40–50 minutes.

**From train station (Ga Ha Noi):** 2 km south. Grab 40,000–60,000 VND or 20-minute walk north along Pho Hue.

**From within city:** Walk or Grab. Traffic is dense; motorbike taxis (xe om) 20,000–40,000 VND for short hops.

## Highlights not to miss

- **Hang Bac (Silver Street):** Still has silver shops. Watch artisans work at the back of stores.
- **Hang Gai (Silk Street):** Tailors and silk shops. Bargain hard; quality varies.
- **Hang Ma (Paper Offerings Street):** Colored paper, lanterns, festival decorations. Most photogenic at night.
- **Ma May Street:** Restored traditional house (Heritage House 87 Ma May) — 10,000 VND entry, shows 19th-century merchant home.
- **Ta Hien (Beer Street):** Plastic stools, draft beer (bia hoi) at 10,000–15,000 VND/glass. Loud, crowded, fun.
- **Dong Xuan Market:** Largest indoor market. Wholesale upstairs, retail ground floor. Bargain aggressively.

## Suggested walking route (2–3 hours)

1. **Start:** Hoan Kiem Lake north shore → cross into Hang Ngang
2. **Hang Bac** (silver) → **Hang Gai** (silk) → **Hang Ma** (paper)
3. **Ma May** → Heritage House 87 (5 min visit)
4. **Ta Hien** → beer street photo stop
5. **Dong Xuan Market** → browse, snack
6. **Hang Dao** → night market if Fri–Sun evening
7. **End:** Back to lake for sunset

**Tip:** Go before 08:00 for empty streets and soft light. After 10:00 it gets crowded.

## Practical tips

- **Money:** ATMs on Hang Bac and Hang Gai. Carry small bills (10,000–50,000 VND) for street food.
- **Traffic:** Motorbikes dominate. Look both ways, walk steadily — don't stop mid-crossing.
- **Pickpockets:** Rare but present in crowds. Front pockets, zipped bags.
- **Bargaining:** Start at 50% of asking price for souvenirs. Fixed prices in cafes and restaurants.
- **Heat:** May–Aug is brutal. Walk 06:00–08:00, hide 11:00–16:00.
- **Rain:** Oct–Nov drizzle. Umbrella or rain jacket essential.

## Frequently asked questions

**Is the Old Quarter safe at night?**
Yes. Streets are well-lit and busy until 23:00. Watch for motorbikes on narrow lanes, not people.

**How much time do I need?**
2–3 hours for a walking loop. Add 1 hour for night market (Fri–Sun). Full day if you include museums nearby.

**Can I walk the whole Old Quarter in one day?**
Yes, but it's dense. Break into morning (eastern streets) and evening (western streets + night market).

**Is the night market worth it?**
For atmosphere, yes. For shopping, prices are inflated. Go for food and photos, not deals.

**Do I need a guide?**
No. The grid is small and streets are named. A self-guided walk with a map app works well.
