# Marble Mountains: Five Peaks, Caves, and Pagodas

The Marble Mountains (Ngu Hanh Son) are five limestone and marble hills 7 km south of Da Nang city center. Each peak is named after one of the five elements: Thuy (Water), Moc (Wood), Hoa (Fire), Kim (Metal), and Tho (Earth). The most visited is Thuy Son (Water Mountain), which has caves, pagodas, and a panoramic viewpoint.

The mountains are a mix of natural caves (some with Buddhist shrines inside) and man-made pagodas. The caves are cool, dark, and atmospheric — a contrast to the hot, bright coast nearby.

## Location & getting there

The Marble Mountains are 7 km south of Da Nang city center, on the road to Hoi An.

**From Da Nang center:** 15–20 min by Grab (80,000–120,000 VND) or bus 1 (20,000 VND, 30 min).

**From Hoi An:** 20 km north. Grab 200,000–300,000 VND, 30 min.

**From Da Nang airport:** 8 km. Grab 100,000–150,000 VND, 15 min.

## Highlights not to miss

- **Huyen Khong Cave:** The largest cave on Thuy Son. A natural skylight lets sunlight stream in. Buddhist shrine inside. Atmospheric and photogenic.
- **Tang Chon Cave:** Smaller cave with a natural window overlooking the coast. Good photo spot.
- **Linh Ung Pagoda:** At the summit of Thuy Son. Panoramic view of Da Nang, the coast, and the other four peaks.
- **Xa Loi Tower:** Seven-story pagoda near the entrance. Visible from the road.
- **Am Phu Cave (Hell Cave):** Separate entrance, 20,000 VND. Depicts Buddhist hell with statues. Dark, atmospheric, slightly eerie.
- **Stone carving village:** At the base. Marble statues and souvenirs. Bargain hard.

## Suggested visit plan (1.5–2.5 hours)

1. **07:00–07:30:** Arrive. Take elevator up (15,000 VND) or walk 150 steps.
2. **07:30–08:15:** Huyen Khong Cave — the highlight. Sunlight through skylight.
3. **08:15–08:45:** Walk to summit. Linh Ung Pagoda. Panoramic view.
4. **08:45–09:15:** Tang Chon Cave + other caves on the way down.
5. **09:15–09:30:** Am Phu Cave (optional, 20,000 VND). Stone carving village at base.

**Tip:** Go at 07:00. By 09:30 it's hot and crowded. The caves are cool but the paths are exposed.

## Practical tips

- **Footwear:** Non-slip shoes. Steps are steep and can be slippery. Sandals risky.
- **Clothing:** Light, breathable. The caves are cool (bring a light jacket for Huyen Khong).
- **Heat:** May–Aug midday is brutal. Go early. The elevator helps.
- **Money:** Entry 40,000 VND + elevator 15,000 VND + Am Phu 20,000 VND = 75,000 VND total. Cash only.
- **Bargaining:** Stone carving village at base. Start at 50% of asking price.
- **Accessibility:** Elevator to the top. Paths between caves have steps. Not fully wheelchair accessible.
- **Children:** Caves are fun for kids but dark. Bring a phone flashlight.

## Frequently asked questions

**Is the elevator worth it?**
Yes in heat (May–Aug). In cool weather (Nov–Feb), the walk up is fine and the views are better.

**How long do I need?**
1.5–2.5 hours for Thuy Son (main peak). Add 30 min for Am Phu Cave.

**Can I visit all 5 peaks?**
Only Thuy Son is fully developed for visitors. The other 4 have limited access and are less interesting. Focus on Thuy Son.

**Is it suitable for children?**
Yes. Caves are fun. Steps can be steep — hold hands. Bring a flashlight for dark caves.

**Is Am Phu Cave worth the extra 20,000 VND?**
Yes if you're interested in Buddhist hell imagery. It's atmospheric and unique. Skip if you're short on time.
