# Fansipan Mountain: The Roof of Indochina at 3,143 m

Fansipan is the highest peak in Vietnam, Laos, and Cambodia — 3,143 m above sea level. It sits in the Hoang Lien Son range, 9 km from Sapa town. The mountain has two faces: the cable car face (tourist, accessible, 15 minutes) and the trekking face (2 days, physical, rewarding). Both reach the same summit.

The cable car (Sun World Fansipan Legend) is 6,292.5 m long — one of the longest three-wire cable cars in the world. The trekking route is 12–15 km over two days with a night in a mountain hut.

## Location & getting there

Fansipan is 9 km southwest of Sapa town center, in Hoang Lien Son National Park.

**From Sapa town:**
- **Cable car:** 9 km by road to the station (15–20 min by taxi, 50,000–80,000 VND). Or free shuttle from Sapa (check at station).
- **Trekking:** Trailhead at Tram Ton (Silver Waterfall area), 15 km from Sapa. Taxi 150,000–200,000 VND.

**From Hanoi:** 5–6 hours by bus/train to Sapa, then 15–20 min to cable car station.

**From Lao Cai (train station):** 1 hour by bus/taxi to Sapa, then 15–20 min to station.

## Highlights not to miss

- **The Summit:** 3,143 m. A pyramidal marker, a small temple, and a 360° view of the Hoang Lien Son range. On clear days, you can see into China.
- **The Cable Car Ride:** 15–20 minutes through clouds and over valleys. The ride itself is an experience — the cable car passes through cloud layers.
- **The Temple Complex:** At the summit, a complex of pagodas and statues (Amitabha Buddha, 21.5 m tall) built in 2016. Impressive scale.
- **Trekking (2 days):** The physical route. Day 1: Tram Ton → camp at 2,800 m. Day 2: summit → descend. Requires guide and permit.
- **Sunrise/Sunset:** The summit is open 24/7 (weather permitting). Sunrise from the top is spectacular but requires an overnight stay near the summit.

## Suggested visit plan (cable car — half day)

1. **07:30–08:00:** Arrive at cable car station. Buy tickets online in advance to skip queue.
2. **08:00–08:20:** Cable car ascent. Pass through cloud layers.
3. **08:20–08:40:** Walk from upper station to summit (600 steps or summit tram).
4. **08:40–09:30:** Summit — photos, temple, views. Wait for clouds to clear.
5. **09:30–10:00:** Descend. Optional: muong hoa funicular to Sapa.

**Tip:** Go at 07:30 on a weekday. By 09:30 the summit is crowded. Clouds are thickest 10:00–14:00.

## Suggested visit plan (trekking — 2 days)

**Day 1:** Tram Ton trailhead (08:00) → 6 hours hiking → camp at 2,800 m (tent + sleeping bag provided by guide).
**Day 2:** 04:00 summit push for sunrise → 3,143 m → descend to Tram Ton by 14:00.

**Note:** Requires guide (mandatory), permit, and good fitness. Not for beginners.

## Practical tips

- **Weather:** Summit is 5–10°C cooler than Sapa. Bring a jacket even in summer. Clouds can obscure views — check forecast.
- **Altitude:** 3,143 m. Some people feel mild altitude effects (headache, breathlessness). Ascend slowly, hydrate.
- **Clothing:** Layers. Wind jacket, fleece, long pants. The cable car and summit are windy.
- **Photography:** No drones (prohibited). Tripods allowed. Best light: 07:30–09:00 and 16:00–17:00.
- **Accessibility:** Cable car is wheelchair accessible. Summit has steps (600 from upper station). Summit tram available (70,000 VND).
- **Children:** Cable car is fine for all ages. Trekking: not recommended under 12.

## Frequently asked questions

**Is the cable car worth it?**
Yes. The view from the summit is the best in Vietnam. The cable car ride itself is scenic. Worth the price.

**Can I trek Fansipan without a guide?**
No. Guides are mandatory for trekking. Permit required. Book through a Sapa agency.

**What if it's cloudy at the summit?**
Clouds are common. Go early (07:30) for the best chance of clear views. Even in clouds, the temple and atmosphere are impressive.

**Is Fansipan suitable for children?**
Cable car: yes, all ages. Trekking: not recommended under 12.

**How cold is it at the summit?**
5–10°C cooler than Sapa town. In winter (Dec–Feb), it can be 0–5°C with frost. Bring layers.
