# War Remnants Museum: The War Seen from the Other Side

The War Remnants Museum is the most visited museum in Ho Chi Minh City and one of the most visited in Vietnam. It documents the Vietnam War (called the "American War" in Vietnam) from the Vietnamese perspective. The exhibits are graphic, emotional, and unflinching — photos of civilian casualties, napalm effects, and the lasting impact of Agent Orange.

The museum is not neutral. It is a Vietnamese government institution and presents a clear narrative. Visitors should come prepared for strong content and form their own conclusions.

## Location & getting there

The museum is in District 3, 1 km north of Ben Thanh Market.

**From Ben Thanh Market:** 12-min walk north along Le Quy Don → Vo Van Tan. Or Grab 30,000–50,000 VND.

**From airport:** 7 km. Taxi/Grab 150,000–250,000 VND, 20–30 min.

**From District 1 hotels:** Walk or Grab. Most central hotels are within 2 km.

## Highlights not to miss

- **Outdoor military equipment:** US tanks, helicopters, artillery, and a fighter jet. Good for photos. Kids often enjoy this section.
- **"Requiem" photo exhibition:** Pulitzer Prize-winning photos by war photographers. The most powerful section. Graphic content — not for children.
- **Agent Orange exhibit:** Documents the effects of dioxin on Vietnamese civilians. Includes photos of birth defects and contaminated areas. Very heavy.
- **Tiger Cages:** Replica of the prison cells used on Con Dao island. Shows conditions for political prisoners.
- **"War Crimes" exhibition:** Documents alleged US and South Vietnamese military actions. Presented from Vietnamese perspective.
- **Guillotine:** Used by French colonial forces. Displayed in the courtyard.

## Suggested visit plan (1.5–2.5 hours)

1. **07:30–08:00:** Arrive early. Outdoor military equipment first (cool, good light).
2. **08:00–08:30:** "Requiem" photo exhibition (most impactful, do it fresh).
3. **08:30–09:00:** Agent Orange exhibit. Heavy — take breaks.
4. **09:00–09:30:** Tiger Cages and "War Crimes" exhibition.
5. **09:30–10:00:** Gift shop, cafe, rest.

**Tip:** Go early and allow time to process. This is not a quick photo stop.

## Practical tips

- **Content warning:** Graphic photos of war casualties, birth defects, and suffering. Not suitable for children under 12. Adults should prepare emotionally.
- **Perspective:** The museum presents a Vietnamese government narrative. It is not neutral. Read critically and form your own views.
- **Audio guide:** 50,000 VND. Worth it for context on each exhibit.
- **Photography:** No flash in galleries. Outdoor photos fine.
- **Heat:** May–Aug midday is hot. Go early. AC inside galleries.
- **Accessibility:** Ground floor and outdoor area accessible. Upper floors have stairs (no elevator in some sections).
- **Souvenirs:** Gift shop sells books, postcards, and replicas. Prices fixed.

## Frequently asked questions

**Is the War Remnants Museum suitable for children?**
The outdoor military equipment is fine for children. The photo galleries (especially Agent Orange and "Requiem") are graphic and not suitable for children under 12.

**How long do I need?**
1.5–2.5 hours. Allow extra time if you want to read all captions.

**Is the museum biased?**
Yes. It presents a Vietnamese government perspective on the war. It is not neutral. Visitors should be aware of this.

**Can I take photos?**
Yes, without flash in galleries. Outdoor photos fine.

**Is there an audio guide?**
Yes, 50,000 VND at the gate. Available in multiple languages.
