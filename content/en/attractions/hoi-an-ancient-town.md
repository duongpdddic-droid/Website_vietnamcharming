# Hoi An Ancient Town: Where the River Still Remembers

Hoi An Ancient Town is a UNESCO World Heritage Site and one of the best-preserved trading ports in Southeast Asia. Its streets were laid out in the 15th century for merchants from China, Japan, and Europe. The buildings survived because the town declined economically after the Thu Bon River silted up in the 19th century — no one had money to rebuild, so everything stayed original.

Today, 1,107 timber-frame buildings line the streets. Lanterns hang from every shop. Tailors work in open doorways. The river still runs through the center, and on full moon nights, the town turns off electric lights and floats lanterns on the water.

## Location & getting there

Hoi An Ancient Town is on the Thu Bon River, 30 km south of Da Nang.

**From Da Nang airport:** 45–60 min by taxi/Grab (300,000–450,000 VND) or shuttle bus (120,000–150,000 VND).

**From Da Nang city:** 45 min by Grab (250,000–350,000 VND) or local bus 1 (20,000 VND, 1 hour).

**From Hoi An bus station:** 2 km north. Grab 30,000–50,000 VND or 15-min walk.

## Highlights not to miss

- **Japanese Covered Bridge (Chua Cau):** The icon of Hoi An. Built 1593 by Japanese merchants. 18 m long, covered, with a small temple inside. Included in the ticket.
- **Tan Ky House:** 200-year-old merchant home. Shows the Chinese-Vietnamese-Japanese architectural blend. Included in ticket.
- **Phuc Kien Assembly Hall:** Largest Chinese assembly hall. Dedicated to Thien Hau (sea goddess). Free entry (donation box).
- **Lantern-lit streets:** Best at 17:30–19:00 when lanterns light up but before crowds peak. Full moon nights are special.
- **Tailor shops:** 400+ tailors. Suits in 24 hours, dresses in 12. Quality varies — read reviews, check fabric before paying.
- **Thu Bon River:** Sunset boat ride (150,000–200,000 VND/boat, 4 people). Best at 17:30.

## Suggested visit plan (2–3 hours)

1. **16:00–16:30:** Enter town. Walk Tran Phu street (main east-west). Photo stops.
2. **16:30–17:00:** Japanese Covered Bridge (ticket). 10-min visit.
3. **17:00–17:30:** Tan Ky House + Phuc Kien Assembly Hall. 10 min each.
4. **17:30–18:00:** River walk. Sunset photo from An Hoi bridge.
5. **18:00–19:00:** Lantern boat ride (150,000–200,000 VND/boat). Float a lantern.
6. **19:00–20:00:** Night market on An Hoi island. Street food, souvenirs.

**Tip:** The town is most magical at 17:30–18:30. After 19:30 it's packed.

## Practical tips

- **Money:** ATMs on Tran Phu and Nguyen Thai Hoc. Carry small bills for street food and boat rides.
- **Bargaining:** Tailors and souvenir shops expect negotiation. Start at 60% of asking price. Fixed prices in cafes.
- **Tailor quality:** Check fabric before paying deposit. Ask for a fitting. Good tailors: Yaly Couture, A Dong Silk, Bebe.
- **Heat:** Mar–Aug is hot and humid. Walk 16:00–19:00. Midday: hide in cafes.
- **Rain:** Oct–Dec. Umbrella essential. Streets flood occasionally.
- **Lantern full moon:** 14th day of lunar month. Town turns off lights 19:00–21:00. Book accommodation early.

## Frequently asked questions

**Is the 120,000 VND ticket worth it?**
Yes. It includes 5 heritage sites. Pick Japanese Covered Bridge, Tan Ky House, and Phuc Kien Assembly Hall at minimum.

**How long do I need?**
2–3 hours for the town loop. Add 1 hour for tailor fitting. Full day if you include An Bang Beach.

**Can I walk the town at night?**
Yes. Streets are lit by lanterns. Safe and atmospheric. Peak crowds 19:00–21:00.

**Are tailor shops open on Sundays?**
Most are. Some close early. Check hours in advance if you need a rush order.

**Is Hoi An good for families?**
Yes. Flat, walkable, lanterns fascinate children. Avoid midday heat.
