# Po Nagar Cham Towers: 1,200 Years of Hindu Heritage

Po Nagar Cham Towers are a complex of Hindu temples built by the Cham people between the 7th and 12th centuries. The main tower (28 m tall) is dedicated to Yan Po Nagar, the goddess of the country. The complex sits on a hill overlooking the Cai River and Nha Trang city.

The towers are the best-preserved Cham architecture in central Vietnam. They are still used for worship — locals and Cham descendants pray here, especially during the Po Nagar Festival (April–May).

## Location & getting there

Po Nagar is 2 km north of Nha Trang city center, on a hill overlooking the Cai River.

**From Nha Trang center:** 10-min Grab (40,000–60,000 VND) or 25-min walk north along Tran Phu → 2/4 street.

**From airport (Cam Ranh):** 40 km south. Taxi/Grab 300,000–400,000 VND, 45–60 min.

**From VinWonders cable car station:** 5 km north. Grab 80,000–120,000 VND, 12 min.

## Highlights not to miss

- **Main tower (Thap Chinh):** 28 m tall, dedicated to Yan Po Nagar. The interior has a linga (phallic symbol) and a statue of the goddess. The brickwork is original — no mortar, just precise fitting.
- **Northwest tower:** The oldest (7th century). Smaller, dedicated to Cri Cambhu (fertility god).
- **South tower:** Dedicated to Sandhaka (fire god). Partially ruined.
- **East tower:** Dedicated to Ganesh (elephant god). Restored.
- **View from the hill:** The complex overlooks the Cai River and Nha Trang city. Best at sunrise or sunset.
- **Cham dance performances:** During the Po Nagar Festival (April–May). Traditional music and dance.

## Suggested visit plan (30–45 minutes)

1. **07:00–07:15:** Arrive. Walk up the hill. View from the top.
2. **07:15–07:30:** Main tower — remove shoes, enter, see the linga and statue.
3. **07:30–07:45:** Other towers — northwest (oldest), south, east.
4. **07:45–08:00:** Small museum. Photos from the hilltop.

**Tip:** Go at 07:00 for cool air, soft light, and empty towers. By 09:00 tour groups arrive.

## Practical tips

- **Dress:** Shoulders and knees covered. Remove shoes at tower entrances. No hats inside.
- **Photography:** No flash inside towers. Outdoor photos fine. Best light 07:00–08:30.
- **Money:** Entry 22,000 VND (cash). Small shop at entrance sells souvenirs and drinks.
- **Bargaining:** Not applicable — fixed price entry. Souvenir vendors at base: bargain.
- **Heat:** May–Aug midday is hot. The hill has limited shade. Go early.
- **Accessibility:** Steps to the top (no elevator). Not wheelchair accessible.
- **Respect:** This is an active worship site. Speak quietly, don't touch altars.

## Frequently asked questions

**Is Po Nagar worth visiting?**
Yes. It is the best Cham architecture in central Vietnam and still an active worship site. 30–45 minutes is enough.

**How old are the towers?**
The oldest (northwest tower) dates to the 7th century. The main tower is 12th century. Over 1,200 years old.

**Can I enter the towers?**
Yes. Remove shoes and dress modestly. The interior is small — a few people at a time.

**Is there a dress code?**
Yes. Shoulders and knees covered. No hats inside. Shoes off at entrance.

**When is the Po Nagar Festival?**
April–May (lunar calendar). Traditional Cham dance, music, and ceremonies. Very crowded but culturally rich.
