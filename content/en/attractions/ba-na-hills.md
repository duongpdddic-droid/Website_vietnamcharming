# Ba Na Hills: Golden Bridge, Cable Car & French Village

Ba Na Hills is a hill station 1,487 m above sea level, 30 km west of Da Nang. It is a resort complex with a cable car, a French-themed village, a golden bridge held by giant stone hands, and an indoor theme park. It is the most visited attraction in central Vietnam.

The Golden Bridge (Cau Vang) is the headline: a 150 m pedestrian bridge held by two giant stone hands, with panoramic views of the mountains. It opened in 2018 and became an Instagram phenomenon.

## Location & getting there

Ba Na Hills is 30 km west of Da Nang city center, in Hoa Vang District.

**From Da Nang center:** 45–60 min by taxi/Grab (300,000–450,000 VND) or shuttle bus (120,000–150,000 VND). Most hotels arrange transport.

**From Hoi An:** 60–75 min by taxi/Grab (400,000–550,000 VND).

**From Da Nang airport:** 50–60 min by taxi (350,000–500,000 VND).

## Highlights not to miss

- **Golden Bridge (Cau Vang):** 150 m pedestrian bridge held by two giant stone hands. Panoramic mountain views. Best at 07:30–08:30 (empty) or 16:00–17:00 (golden light). Most crowded 09:00–14:00.
- **Cable car:** 5,801 m, 17 minutes. World record for longest single-wire cable car. Passes through cloud layers. Views of mountains and forest.
- **French Village:** Replica French town with cathedral, square, and cafes. Cobblestone streets, European architecture. Good for photos.
- **Fantasy Park:** Indoor theme park with rides, 4D cinema, and games. Included in ticket. Good for families and rainy days.
- **Linh Ung Pagoda:** 27 m tall Buddha statue at the summit. Panoramic view. Peaceful contrast to the busy village.
- **Le Jardin D'Amour:** Flower gardens with themed sections. Best in spring (Mar–May).

## Suggested visit plan (full day)

1. **07:30–08:00:** Arrive. Cable car ascent (17 min). Go straight to Golden Bridge before crowds.
2. **08:00–09:00:** Golden Bridge — photos, views. Walk the full length.
3. **09:00–10:00:** French Village — explore, coffee, photos.
4. **10:00–11:30:** Linh Ung Pagoda + Le Jardin D'Amour.
5. **11:30–13:00:** Buffet lunch (optional) or cafes.
6. **13:00–15:00:** Fantasy Park — rides, games.
7. **15:00–16:00:** Return to Golden Bridge for afternoon light (less crowded).
8. **16:00–17:00:** Cable car descent.

**Tip:** The Golden Bridge is empty at 07:30–08:30. By 09:30 it's packed. Go first thing.

## Practical tips

- **Tickets:** Buy online in advance to skip the queue. Weekend tickets sell out.
- **Weather:** Ba Na Hills is 5–10°C cooler than Da Nang. Bring a jacket. Clouds and fog are common — check forecast.
- **Crowds:** Weekends and holidays are extremely crowded. Go on a weekday if possible.
- **Food:** Buffet lunch (250,000–350,000 VND) is overpriced. Cafes in French Village are better value.
- **Photography:** Golden Bridge is best at 07:30–08:30 (empty) or 16:00–17:00 (golden light). Tripods not allowed on the bridge.
- **Accessibility:** Cable car and French Village are wheelchair accessible. Golden Bridge has steps at both ends.
- **Children:** Fantasy Park is great for kids. Golden Bridge and cable car suit all ages.

## Frequently asked questions

**Is Ba Na Hills worth the price?**
Yes, if you want the Golden Bridge and cable car experience. 900,000 VND is expensive but includes everything. Skip if you only want the bridge photo.

**How long do I need?**
5–8 hours for a full visit. Half-day (4 hours) if you skip Fantasy Park.

**What if it's cloudy?**
Clouds are common. The cable car passes through them. The Golden Bridge may be foggy. Check forecast and go early.

**Is the Golden Bridge crowded?**
Extremely after 09:00. Go at 07:30–08:30 for empty photos.

**Can I stay overnight?**
Yes. Mercure Danang French Village Ba Na Hills hotel is on-site. Expensive but unique.
