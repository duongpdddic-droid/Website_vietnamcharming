# Ben Thanh Market: Saigon's Loudest 100 Years

Ben Thanh Market is the most famous market in Ho Chi Minh City and arguably the most visited. Built in 1914, it sits in the heart of District 1, surrounded by the city's busiest streets. Inside: 1,500 stalls selling food, textiles, souvenirs, dried goods, and everything in between. Outside: a night market that takes over the surrounding streets from 19:00.

This guide covers what to buy, what to eat, how to bargain, and the small scams to avoid.

## Location & getting there

Ben Thanh Market is at the intersection of Le Loi, Ham Nghi, Tran Hung Dao, and Le Lai streets in District 1.

**From Tan Son Nhat airport:** 7 km. Taxi/Grab 150,000–250,000 VND, 20–30 min. Bus 109 (yellow) 20,000 VND, 40 min.

**From District 1 hotels:** Walk or Grab. Most central hotels are within 1 km.

**Metro:** Line 1 (Ben Thanh station) — opening soon. Check current status.

## Highlights not to miss

- **Food section (south side):** Pho, banh mi, com tam, fresh fruit, che (sweet soup). Prices 30,000–80,000 VND. Eat where locals eat — stalls with queues.
- **Dried goods (north side):** Dried mango, squid, shrimp, spices. Good souvenirs. Bargain hard.
- **Textiles (east side):** Silk, ao dai fabric, embroidery. Quality varies — check thread count and weave.
- **Souvenirs (center):** Lanterns, lacquerware, coffee, conical hats. Prices inflated — bargain.
- **Night market (outside):** Clothing, accessories, street food. More relaxed than inside. Better for photos.

## Suggested visit plan (1–2 hours)

1. **07:00–07:30:** Arrive early. Walk the perimeter outside first (photo of clock tower).
2. **07:30–08:30:** Enter through south entrance (food side). Breakfast at a busy stall.
3. **08:30–09:30:** Browse textiles and dried goods. Bargain.
4. **09:30–10:00:** Souvenir shopping. Compare prices across 3 stalls before buying.
5. **Evening return:** 19:00–21:00 for night market. Street food, atmosphere, photos.

**Tip:** The market is hottest and most crowded 11:00–15:00. Avoid midday.

## Practical tips

- **Bargaining:** Start at 40–50% of asking price for souvenirs and textiles. Walk away if the price doesn't drop. Fixed prices for food.
- **Scams:** Watch for "helpful" locals who guide you to specific stalls for commission. Politely decline.
- **Pickpockets:** Rare inside but present in crowds. Front pockets, zipped bags.
- **Quality:** Check textiles for loose threads, souvenirs for defects. Some goods are mass-produced.
- **Payment:** Cash preferred. Some stalls accept cards but with 3–5% surcharge.
- **ATMs:** Outside on Le Loi street (Vietcombank, BIDV). Inside: none.

## Frequently asked questions

**Is Ben Thanh Market a tourist trap?**
Partly. Prices are inflated for tourists, but the food is genuine and the atmosphere is real. Bargain hard and you'll get fair prices.

**What should I buy?**
Dried fruit, coffee, lacquerware, and conical hats are good souvenirs. Avoid "silk" that feels synthetic.

**Is the food safe?**
Yes, at busy stalls with high turnover. Avoid stalls with no customers. Drink bottled water.

**Can I use credit cards?**
Some stalls accept cards with 3–5% surcharge. Cash is better for bargaining.

**Is the night market better than the day market?**
Different. Night market is more relaxed, better for street food and photos. Day market has more variety and better prices.
