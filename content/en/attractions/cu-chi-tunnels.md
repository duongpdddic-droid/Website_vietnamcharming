# Cu Chi Tunnels: 250 km of Underground War History

The Cu Chi Tunnels are a 250 km network of underground tunnels used by Viet Cong soldiers during the Vietnam War. Dug by hand from 1948 to the 1960s, the tunnels included living quarters, hospitals, kitchens, and command centers — all underground, some as deep as 10 m. The tunnels were so well-hidden that US forces could not find them for years.

Today, two sections are open to visitors: **Ben Dinh** (closer to HCMC, more tourist-oriented) and **Ben Duoc** (larger, more authentic, fewer crowds). Both offer tunnel crawling, shooting ranges, and exhibits.

## Location & getting there

Cu Chi Tunnels are 40 km northwest of Ho Chi Minh City center.

**Ben Dinh (closer, more touristy):**
- **From HCMC center:** 1.5 hours by car. Grab 500,000–700,000 VND. Or bus 13 from Ben Thanh → Cu Chi, then taxi 150,000 VND.
- **Tour:** Half-day organized tours 350,000–600,000 VND (includes transport + guide + entry).

**Ben Duoc (larger, more authentic):**
- **From HCMC center:** 2 hours by car. Grab 600,000–800,000 VND.
- **Less crowded.** Better for history enthusiasts.

**Recommendation:** Ben Duoc for authenticity, Ben Dinh for convenience. Most tours go to Ben Dinh.

## Highlights not to miss

- **Tunnel crawling:** Enter a 20–30 m section of tunnel. Narrow (60 cm wide, 80 cm high). Dark, hot, claustrophobic. Not for everyone — you can exit early.
- **Booby traps exhibit:** Replicas of punji sticks, tripwires, and camouflaged pits. Explains how Viet Cong defended the tunnels.
- **Living quarters:** Underground kitchen, hospital, meeting room. Shows how people lived for months underground.
- **Bomb craters:** Still visible on the surface — 500 lb bomb craters from B-52 strikes.
- **Shooting range (optional):** Fire an AK-47 or M16. 50,000–100,000 VND per bullet, 10-bullet minimum. Very loud.
- **Documentary film:** 15-min film at the visitor center. Good context before entering.

## Suggested visit plan (2–3 hours)

1. **07:00–07:30:** Arrive. Watch documentary film at visitor center.
2. **07:30–08:00:** Walk the surface trail — bomb craters, booby trap exhibits, living quarters.
3. **08:00–08:30:** Tunnel crawl (20–30 m). Exit at the other end.
4. **08:30–09:00:** Shooting range (optional) or rest area.
5. **09:00–09:30:** Gift shop, return transport.

**Tip:** Go early. By 10:00 it's hot and crowded. The tunnel crawl is harder in heat.

## Practical tips

- **Clothing:** Long pants and closed shoes. The tunnel is dirty and narrow. Shorts and sandals are uncomfortable.
- **Claustrophobia:** The tunnel crawl is tight and dark. You can exit at any point. Don't force it.
- **Heat:** May–Aug is brutal. Go early, bring water.
- **Mosquitoes:** Jungle setting. Repellent recommended.
- **Children:** The tunnel crawl may be too tight for children under 8. Surface exhibits are fine.
- **Accessibility:** Surface trail is flat but unpaved. Tunnel crawl not accessible.

## Frequently asked questions

**Is the tunnel crawl safe?**
Yes. The crawl section is reinforced and lit. Guides accompany groups. You can exit at any point.

**Which site is better — Ben Dinh or Ben Duoc?**
Ben Duoc is larger and less crowded. Ben Dinh is closer and more tourist-oriented. History enthusiasts prefer Ben Duoc.

**Can I fire a gun?**
Yes, at the shooting range (optional). AK-47, M16, M60 available. 50,000–100,000 VND per bullet, 10-bullet minimum.

**How long do I need?**
2–3 hours per site. Half-day tour from HCMC includes transport.

**Is it suitable for children?**
Surface exhibits yes. Tunnel crawl: children under 8 may find it too tight. Use discretion.
