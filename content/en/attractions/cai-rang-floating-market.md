# Cai Rang Floating Market: The Mekong Delta's Morning Rush

Cai Rang Floating Market is the largest floating market in the Mekong Delta. Every morning from 05:00, hundreds of boats gather on the Can Tho River to buy and sell fruit, vegetables, and goods. The market peaks at 06:00–07:00 and winds down by 09:00.

The market is not a tourist show — it is a working wholesale market where farmers and traders meet on the water. Boats are loaded with pineapples, watermelons, coconuts, and vegetables. Smaller boats weave between them selling breakfast (pho, coffee, bun rieu) to the traders.

## Location & getting there

Cai Rang Floating Market is on the Can Tho River, 6 km south of Can Tho city center.

**From Can Tho center:** 15-min taxi/Grab to Ninh Kieu pier (50,000–80,000 VND), then boat to market (15–20 min).

**From Ho Chi Minh City:** 3.5–4 hours by bus (150,000–200,000 VND) or 45-min flight to Can Tho airport + 20-min taxi.

**From Phu Quoc:** Fly to Can Tho (1 hour) or bus + ferry (6–8 hours).

## Highlights not to miss

- **The market boats:** Large wooden boats loaded with fruit and vegetables. Each boat specializes in one product — look for the "sample pole" (cay beo) showing what they sell.
- **Breakfast boats:** Small boats selling pho, bun rieu, coffee, and banh mi to traders. Pull up alongside and eat on the water.
- **Fruit boats:** Boats selling cut pineapple, mango, and coconut. Buy directly from the boat — 20,000–40,000 VND.
- **Small canal tour:** After the market, take a smaller boat through the narrow canals (rong) behind the market. See fruit orchards, stilt houses, and rural life.
- **Noodle factory:** Many tours include a stop at a small rice noodle (hu tieu) factory. Watch the process and taste fresh noodles.

## Suggested visit plan (2–3 hours)

1. **05:00–05:30:** Depart from Ninh Kieu pier. Boat to market (15–20 min).
2. **05:30–06:30:** Market peak. Cruise between boats. Buy fruit. Eat breakfast on the water.
3. **06:30–07:30:** Small canal tour. See orchards and rural life.
4. **07:30–08:00:** Noodle factory stop (optional). Return to pier.

**Tip:** The market is most active 05:30–07:00. Arriving at 07:30 means you'll see the tail end.

## Practical tips

- **Wake up early:** The market starts at 05:00. Your alarm should be 04:30.
- **Boat booking:** Book through your hotel or at Ninh Kieu pier the evening before. Shared boats are cheaper; private boats give more flexibility.
- **Money:** Cash only. Small bills for fruit and breakfast.
- **Sun protection:** Hat, sunscreen. The boat is open. By 07:00 it's hot.
- **Rain:** May–Oct. Bring rain jacket or umbrella. Boats have plastic covers.
- **Photography:** Early morning light is best. The market is most photogenic 05:30–06:30. No tripods on boats.
- **Etiquette:** This is a working market. Don't obstruct boats or touch goods without buying. Ask before photographing people.

## Frequently asked questions

**Is Cai Rang Floating Market a tourist trap?**
No. It is a real working market. Tourists are welcome but should respect that it's a workplace, not a performance.

**What time should I arrive?**
05:00–05:30 for the peak. By 07:30 the market is winding down.

**Can I visit without a boat tour?**
No. The market is on the river. Boat access only.

**How long do I need?**
2–3 hours total (including boat ride and canal tour).

**Is it suitable for children?**
Yes. The boat ride is fun. Bring life jackets for young children (provided by most operators).
