# VinWonders Nha Trang: Theme Park on an Island

VinWonders Nha Trang (formerly Vinpearl Land) is a large theme park on Hon Tre Island, 3 km off the coast of Nha Trang. It combines a theme park, water park, aquarium, and beach club — all accessible by a 3.3 km cable car over the sea. It is the most popular family attraction in Nha Trang.

The cable car ride alone is worth the trip. The park is big enough for a full day and has enough variety for all ages.

## Location & getting there

VinWonders is on Hon Tre Island, 3 km from Nha Trang city.

**From Nha Trang center:**
- **Cable car:** 10-min Grab to cable car station (An Vien area), then 12–15 min cable car to island. Total 30–40 min.
- **Speedboat:** 15 min from Nha Trang port. 100,000–150,000 VND one way.

**From airport (Cam Ranh):** 40 km south. Taxi/Grab 300,000–400,000 VND, 45–60 min to cable car station.

## Highlights not to miss

- **Cable car:** 3.3 km over the sea. The ride itself is a highlight. Best in morning light.
- **Theme park:** Roller coasters, Ferris wheel, carousel, and family rides. Good for all ages.
- **Water park:** Slides, wave pool, lazy river. Best in summer. Bring swimsuit.
- **Aquarium:** Underwater tunnel with sharks, rays, and tropical fish. Good for kids.
- **Beach club:** Private beach on Hon Tre Island. Swimming, sunbathing, water sports.
- **Evening show:** Water and light show at 20:00 (seasonal). Check schedule.

## Suggested visit plan (full day)

1. **08:30–09:00:** Cable car to island. Arrive before crowds.
2. **09:00–11:00:** Theme park — rides, Ferris wheel, photos.
3. **11:00–12:30:** Water park — slides, wave pool.
4. **12:30–13:30:** Lunch (buffet or food court).
5. **13:30–15:00:** Aquarium + beach club.
6. **15:00–17:00:** Free time — re-ride favorites, shopping.
7. **17:00–18:00:** Cable car back. Sunset views.

**Tip:** Go on a weekday. Weekends are extremely crowded. Arrive at opening.

## Practical tips

- **Tickets:** Buy online in advance to skip the queue. Combo tickets (park + buffet) save money.
- **What to bring:** Swimsuit, towel, sunscreen, hat, water shoes. Lockers available (50,000 VND).
- **Food:** Buffet is overpriced. Food court inside is cheaper (80,000–150,000 VND/dish). Or bring snacks.
- **Heat:** May–Aug is hot. Water park and beach are the best places to cool down.
- **Crowds:** Weekends and holidays are packed. Go on a weekday.
- **Accessibility:** Cable car and main paths are wheelchair accessible. Some rides have restrictions.
- **Children:** Water park has a kids' area. Aquarium is great for all ages. Stroller-friendly paths.

## Frequently asked questions

**Is VinWonders worth the price?**
Yes for families and full-day visitors. 880,000 VND is expensive but includes cable car + all areas. Skip if you only want the cable car ride.

**How long do I need?**
Full day (6–8 hours) to see everything. Half-day (4 hours) if you skip water park.

**Is the cable car safe?**
Yes. It operates in most weather. Strong wind may close it — check at station.

**Can I bring food?**
Small snacks allowed. Large coolers not permitted. Food court inside.

**Is it suitable for toddlers?**
Yes. Stroller-friendly paths, kids' water area, aquarium. Cable car is safe for all ages.
