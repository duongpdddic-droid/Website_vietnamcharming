# Phong Nha in 2026: the world's largest cave, and the trips that are actually bookable

UNESCO listed this national park in 2003. The justification sits in the rock: a limestone massif hundreds of millions of years old, pierced by underground rivers that run beneath whole ridges. Inside one of those ridges is Hang Son Doong, the biggest cave passage anyone has measured — tall enough for a skyscraper, with clouds and a jungle of its own.

But Son Doong is not the whole story. Most visitors end up at Paradise Cave, Phong Nha Cave or Dark Cave, which are far easier to reach and cost a fraction of the price. This Phong Nha travel guide separates the three tiers of cave experience, gives current prices, and lays out a three-day plan that matches what you can realistically book.

## What Phong Nha offers

Three tiers. The easy tier is road-and-boat caves: Phong Nha, Paradise, Tien Son, Dark Cave. The middle tier is two-day overnight treks into the jungle, mainly Hang En and Tu Lan. The hard tier is the Son Doong expedition: six days, five nights, USD 3,000.

Beyond caves, there is the Son River, rice fields, Son Trach village and quiet roads for cycling between limestone ridges. Plenty of people stay two nights, take an afternoon boat, eat grilled river fish and leave without entering a single wild cave.

## When to go

| Period | Conditions | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| February–April | 22–30°C, dry, clear river | The most comfortable window |
| May–August | 28–36°C, full jungle, afternoon showers | Good caving, hard heat |
| September–November | Heavy rain, flood risk, some tours suspended | Avoid cave itineraries |
| December–January | 17–22°C, drizzle, damp | Cheap, quiet, thin waterfalls |

The key local rule: rain here floods caves and closes roads, not just your plans for a walk. Between September and November, river caves can shut with little warning. If caves are the reason for the trip, keep a spare day in the plan.

## Son Doong: price, duration and the truth about availability

Oxalis Adventure is the only operator licensed to run Son Doong. The current price is USD 3,000 per person, roughly 80 million VND. The official trip is now **six days and five nights**: two hotel nights before and after, one jungle camp near the cave entrance, and two nights camped inside the cave.

The "four days, three nights" figure you will still see online is out of date, and so is the 16+ age limit; the current minimum is 18, with a maximum of 70. Groups are capped at 10 guests, supported by roughly 25–30 guides, safety staff, cooks and porters.

The physical bar is real: about 28 km of trekking, elevation change up to 800 m, repeated river crossings and around 9 km of movement inside the cave. It is the hardest trip Oxalis runs.

On availability, as of October 2026: 2026 and 2027 are full, and much of 2028 has gone. If Son Doong is the goal, register directly on Oxalis's official site — not through a reseller — and start training six to twelve months out.

## Hang En, Tu Lan and the overnight alternatives

This is the tier that delivers the most value for most travellers: sleep in a cave, trek through jungle, pay a fraction of the Son Doong price.

| Tu Lan Encounter | 2 days / 1 night | about USD 253 (6.7 million VND) | Moderate |
| Hang Va | 2 days / 1 night | about 9.4 million VND | Moderate–hard |
| Hang Tien–Tu Lan Discovery | 2 days / 1 night | about USD 204 (5.4 million VND) | Easy |

Hang En is the third-largest cave passage on earth, with a sandbank and an underground lake inside. It is also the first night of the Son Doong expedition, which means it gives you the closest experience to the big trip at a tenth of the cost.

All four run through Oxalis or another licensed operator. Book on the official site; fake Son Doong booking pages are an active problem, and Oxalis has publicly warned about look-alike domains.

## Paradise Cave, Phong Nha Cave and Dark Cave

These three need no advance booking. Buy at the gate.

**Paradise Cave** has about 31 km of surveyed passage, but visitors walk roughly 1 km on a lit wooden boardwalk. Entry is 250,000 VND. The final platform overlooks a vast chamber; most people turn back too early and miss it.

**Phong Nha Cave** is entered by dragon boat, motoring about 1.5 km into a dark river cave. Entry is 150,000 VND, and the boat costs around 700,000 VND for up to 12 passengers. Afternoon light slanting into the entrance is better than midday.

**Dark Cave** is the activity cave: zipline in, swim, natural mud bath, kayak out. Around 450,000 VND per person including guide and gear. The mud permanently stains white clothing.

**Mooc Spring**, at about 80,000 VND, is a spring-fed swimming channel beside the Ho Chi Minh Road. Go before 10:00, before the coach groups arrive.

## Getting around and the warnings that matter

Dong Hoi airport lies 45 km east, a 50–60 minute drive costing 350,000–450,000 VND in a taxi. North–south trains also stop in Dong Hoi; a soft sleeper from Hanoi eats 9–11 hours.

Ride-hailing does not operate in Son Trach. Options are a rented scooter at 120,000–150,000 VND per day or a taxi called by your guesthouse. Most places lend bicycles free.

Four things to know:

- Cash is essential. The ATMs in Son Trach and Dong Hoi are unreliable, so leave Hanoi or Hue carrying 2–3 million VND.
- Nobody may trek inside the national park without a licensed operator.
- Drones and tripods need permits; rangers enforce a no-fly zone over the core.
- Administratively, the area has belonged to Quang Tri province since the 2025 merger; the airport and station keep the name Dong Hoi.

## Frequently asked questions

**Is Phong Nha worth visiting without Son Doong?**
Absolutely. Paradise and Phong Nha caves are impressive on their own, and Hang En or Tu Lan give you a cave overnight for a fraction of the price.

**How many days do I need?**
Three days covers the accessible caves. Four to five if you add a two-day overnight tour.

**Can I visit Paradise Cave independently?**
Yes. Buy a ticket at the gate and walk the boardwalk; no guide is required.

**When should I avoid Phong Nha?**
September to November, the flood season. River caves close, roads flood and tours are cancelled.

**Is it suitable for children?**
Paradise and Phong Nha caves work for older children. Hang En and Tu Lan require age 16+, Son Doong 18+.
