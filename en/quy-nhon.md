# Quy Nhon: a central Vietnam coastline that has not learned to perform

Quy Nhon already has everything a beach city needs: clear water, fresh seafood, a working fishing economy, a bridge almost 2.5 km long across a lagoon, and rocky coves tucked under the hills. What it does not have is a crowd.

That is its biggest advantage — and the reason to go sooner rather than later. This Quy Nhon travel guide covers the coves, the Cham towers and the food, with the timing that decides whether the sea cooperates.

## Where Quy Nhon is — and which province it belongs to now

Quy Nhon sits on the south-central coast between Hoi An and Nha Trang, roughly 1,100 km from Hanoi and 650 km from Ho Chi Minh City.

> **Administrative update (2026):** on 1 July 2025 Binh Dinh province was merged into Gia Lai. Quy Nhon is now **Quy Nhon ward, Gia Lai province**, and it serves as the province's administrative centre. Older guides still say "Quy Nhon, Binh Dinh" — common, but no longer administratively correct.

The geography splits cleanly in two directions:

- **North** (the Nhon Ly peninsula): Eo Gio, Ky Co, and the Nhon Ly fishing village.
- **South** (Ghenh Rang): the egg-stone beach, Han Mac Tu's grave, small coves under the hills.

## When to go

| Window | Conditions | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| **February – May** | 24–31°C, calm sea, little rain | Snorkelling, photography, boat trips |
| **June – August** | 28–35°C, glassy water | Swimming, families with children |
| **September – November** | Rain builds, typhoon risk | Avoid if your dates are fixed |
| **December – January** | 21–26°C, drizzle, cheapest rates | Budget travellers, anyone wanting quiet |

**Best overall:** March to May. The sea is calm, the light is good, and the domestic summer holidays have not started.

June to August is hotter but the water is at its flattest and clearest. With small children that can actually be the better choice, because the swimming is easier.

## What to see

### Eo Gio

A wind-scoured cape where the sea funnels through a rock gate. A coastal walking path follows the hillside for one to two kilometres, looking down into a green cove.

- Best light: 5:00–6:30 am or 4:30–5:30 pm.
- The wind is genuinely strong; a hat without a strap will not survive.
- Do not swim here — rocks and surge.

### Ky Co

The turquoise crescent below Eo Gio. A road now reaches the cove and there is an entry fee.

- Go before 9 am or after 3 pm. At midday there is no shade and the sand is too hot to stand on.
- Weekends fill with domestic groups early.
- Bring water; facilities inside the cove are limited.

### Ghenh Rang and the egg-stone beach

Rounded boulders at the foot of the hill, with waves pushing into small pools between them. Nearby is Queen Beach, where legend has it Queen Nam Phuong used to bathe.

Only get in the water when the sea is genuinely calm. The rocks are slippery and the surge is real, not a caution invented for signs.

### Han Mac Tu's grave

The poet died in 1940; his grave sits on the Ghenh Rang hill facing the sea. This is a place of pilgrimage for readers of Vietnamese poetry, not a photo stop. Keep quiet and leave nothing behind.

### Thap Doi (the twin towers)

A pair of Cham brick towers inside the city, built around the 12th century. Late afternoon is when the brick holds colour best. Do not climb on the structures.

### Banh It tower

A hilltop tower about 20 km north of the centre, dating to roughly the 11th century. It combines well with the Duong Long towers near Tay Son if you have a full half day.

### Hon Kho island

A small island off Nhon Hai with coral and clear water. Boats leave from Nhon Hai; expect 300,000–500,000 VND per person depending on operator and stops.

Use reef-safe sunscreen. The coral here is already under pressure from tourism.

### Thi Nai bridge

Almost 2.5 km across the Thi Nai lagoon. Best at sunrise, when the fishing boats head out and the lagoon is still flat.

## A three-day plan

| Day | Morning | Afternoon | Evening |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Day 1** | Arrive, check in near the beachfront | Thap Doi, Quy Nhon market, coffee | Seafood by the sea, walk the promenade |
| **Day 2** | 6 am at Eo Gio, then down to Ky Co to swim | Lunch at Nhon Ly fishing village, back by 3 pm | Thi Nai bridge at sunset, banh xeo for dinner |
| **Day 3** | Bun cha ca breakfast, then Ghenh Rang and Han Mac Tu's grave | Bai Xep, or a boat trip to Hon Kho | Night train or flight out |

With an extra day, give a full half day to Banh It and Duong Long. Both are out toward Tay Son, far enough that a private car makes sense.

A useful local rhythm: the good banh xeo stalls open in the late afternoon and evening, while bun cha ca shops open at dawn and often sell out before 10 am.

## Practical notes

- **Getting there:** Phu Cat airport is about 35 km north of the centre, 400,000–550,000 VND by taxi. Trains stop at Dieu Tri, about 10 km west.
- **In the city:** ride-hailing works well. For Eo Gio and Ky Co, hire a scooter (120,000–180,000 VND/day) or a car and driver for the morning.
- **Cash:** ATMs in the city are reliable, but there are very few around Nhon Ly. Withdraw before heading onto the peninsula.
- **Swimming safety:** Eo Gio and Ghenh Rang have rocks and strong surge. Swim at Ky Co and at the city beaches.
- **At Cham sites:** shoulders covered, no climbing on the brickwork, no touching the exhibits.
- **Storm season:** October and November carry real typhoon risk. If you travel then, book accommodation with a flexible cancellation policy.

## FAQ

**How many days do I need in Quy Nhon?**
Three days covers Eo Gio, Ky Co, Ghenh Rang and one proper evening of eating. Four allows Banh It and Duong Long.

**Quy Nhon or Nha Trang?**
Nha Trang has more services and polish. Quy Nhon is quieter, cheaper and feels more local. If you need full resort infrastructure, Nha Trang still wins.

**Should I stay in Nhon Ly to reach Eo Gio at dawn?**
If sunrise photography is the goal, yes. Be aware that evenings there are very quiet with few dining options.

**Is October a good month?**
Risky. It is a transition period, and rain or a typhoon can wipe out a beach itinerary.

**Can children manage Ky Co?**
Yes, but go early and bring shade. The path down to the cove is stepped and steep in places.
